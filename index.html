Who We Are

Where music meets medicine

Sofia V has been playing violin since age 8. During COVID, she started recording videos, and what began as a way to share music became a YouTube channel, then a street performance in Central Park, and now a full-on fundraising mission.

Music 4 Medicine is about the idea that music doesn't just move you emotionally. It can change the material reality of someone's life. Every dollar raised goes directly to Bergen New Bridge Medical Center to keep care accessible for those who need it most.

Music as a force for healing, in every sense of the word.

The Cause

Bergen New Bridge Medical Center

Bergen New Bridge is New Jersey's largest hospital and one of its most important. As a safety net hospital, it serves thousands of patients who have nowhere else to turn: uninsured, underinsured, and those facing poverty, mental illness, and addiction.

These patients deserve quality care just as much as anyone else. Bergen New Bridge makes that possible, and Music 4 Medicine is proud to help them keep doing it.

Safety Net Hospital NJ's Largest Hospital Mental Health Care Addiction Recovery Underserved Patients
Music heals emotionally, but it can also heal physically, by funding the care that makes recovery possible. The heart of Music 4 Medicine
